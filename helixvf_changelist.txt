# Changelist
- The changes are an attempt to balance the vore with the combat to give it a greater presense in the gameplay. 
Allowing for more vore setups without taking too much away from the gameplay. 
/!\ I can't add new model or animations yet. I can only modify what is available /!\

## General Game Changes
- Sprinting will not drain stamina (Eventually want to change it to just out of combat)
- Enemys will be slightly more agressive with attack chains that will usually end in a grab move
- For each enemy, one of the escape routes on will be consistent difficulty and unaffected by agression. Like a weakpoint. It will be unique for each enemy.

## Enemy Changes
### Yama
- Most of Yama's attack chains will end in bite or sprint
- Yama's sprint will now almost always lead to av grab
- At higher agression bite can lead directly to walking belly
- Drain can now be stopped by inputting correct struggle directions. This also lets you stop drain when stamina is set to infinite in sandbox mode.
- In walking belly, Yama now only enters her den at very low agression. If higher agression, she continues to partol the area instead
